runtime_sound_port_semantics
Purpose: non-BBC sound-backend guide derived from Cybertron loader SOUND/ENVELOPE data and runtime $21EA call sites.
Original authority: loader BASIC lines 170-290 ENVELOPE, 1360-1390 PROCsound, 5010-5220 sound DATA; runtime $21EA OSWORD 7 dispatch.
Reference semantics: BBC User Guide SOUND/ENVELOPE and OSWORD A=7; Acorn Electron User Guide Chapter 22 for Electron envelope/channel behavior.

bbc_sound_semantics_for_port
OSWORD A=7 block: channel, amplitude_or_envelope, pitch, duration as four signed little-endian 16-bit values.
channel is decoded as hex nibbles $HSFC: H=hold/continue, S=synchronization group, F=flush, C=channel; C=0 noise, C=1..3 tone channels.
duration_seconds = duration / 20 for duration 0..254; duration -1 means continue until stopped. Cybertron uses duration 0 as a silent flush/stop parameter on ids 12 and 16.
tone_pitch_hz = 440 * 2^((pitch - 89) / 48) for tone channels before pitch-envelope deltas; pitch wraps to 0..255.
positive amplitude selects an envelope number; negative amplitude is fixed loudness where -15 is loudest and 0 is silence.
noise channel pitch classes: 0 high periodic, 1 medium periodic, 2 low periodic, 3 periodic tied to channel 1, 4 high white, 5 medium white, 6 low white, 7 white tied to channel 1.
Pitch-envelope increments are pitch units; one pitch unit is a quarter semitone. Envelope step time is (T & $7F) centiseconds; T bit 7 set means hold final pitch instead of auto-repeating the pitch envelope.

port_rules
Do not map positive amplitudes 5-10 to fixed volume. Cybertron defines envelopes 5-10 in the loader, despite the loader comment about older OS versions.
Preserve flush bit F=1 on ids whose channel_raw is $0010 or $0011; these are immediate effect/stop-style requests, not queued notes.
Treat ids with uses=0 as reserved original table rows until a whole-program proof says otherwise; keep the data available to the port asset table.
For a non-BBC backend, synthesize tone channels as square waves and noise channel classes separately; apply envelope pitch deltas in quarter-semitone units at the exported centisecond step cadence.
